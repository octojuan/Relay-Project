THE PROBLEM

The business case

A large consumer tech company ships an enormous volume of marketing and product content across its entire lineup — and the majority of its sales flow through channel partners, not direct channels.

1,000s
of partners
Majority
of sales via partners

Yet partners were stitching together assets from scattered, inconsistent sources.

Every color, every size, every product line — all needing partner-ready assets.

THE APPROACH

What we're solving

Deliver a best-in-class asset distribution platform for thousands of partners, focused on the partner experience.

  • Speed to market
  • Secure delivery
  • Single source of truth

No partner should have to guess which version of an asset is current.

Every launch, every product line

New colors, new finishes, new sizes — every one needing fresh assets, in every locale, on launch day.

THE PRODUCT

What we shipped

A self-serve partner portal

  • Browse assets by product line
  • Curated "featured" bundles on every release
  • Personal library + one-click add-all

Guardrails built in

  • Brand & trademark guidance surfaced in-flow
  • Production-ready specs, not just raw files
  • Built-in support & FAQ
